SECTION 3: IT GENERAL CONTROLS

Control IT-AC-01 — User Access Provisioning
All new user accounts are created based on a signed access request form approved by the
line manager. Access is provisioned within one business day of receiving an approved request.
Testing result: No exceptions noted across 25 new-hire accounts sampled.

Control IT-AC-02 — Quarterly Access Review
Access rights to all critical systems are reviewed quarterly by the system owner. Accounts
with no login in the prior 90 days are flagged for deactivation. Privileged accounts are
reviewed monthly.
Testing result: No exceptions noted. Last review completed 15 June 2025.

Control IT-AC-03 — Offboarding Procedure
Upon employee termination or role change, HR notifies IT within four business hours.
IT revokes or adjusts access to all systems within one business day of notification.
Evidence: 12 terminations processed in Q2 2025; all access revoked within SLA.
Testing result: No exceptions noted.

Control IT-DM-01 — Data Classification Policy
A data classification policy is maintained and communicated to all staff annually.
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Confidential and
Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Testing result: Policy reviewed and re-issued March 2025. Awareness training completed
by 98% of staff.

Control IT-INC-01 — Security Incident Reporting
All staff are required to report suspected security incidents to the IT Security team via
the internal ticketing portal within 24 hours of discovery. The IT Security team triages
and escalates within four hours.
Testing result: 3 incidents reported in Q2 2025; all triaged within SLA.

SECTION 4: BUSINESS CONTINUITY

Control BC-01 — Business Continuity Plan Maintenance
A business continuity plan is maintained covering critical IT systems and key third-party
service dependencies. The BCP is reviewed annually and approved by the board.
Last review: February 2025.

Control BC-02 — BCP Testing
The BCP is tested annually through a tabletop exercise. The most recent exercise was
conducted in March 2025 and covered scenarios including extended outage of the primary
cloud provider. All recovery time objectives (RTOs) were met during the exercise.
